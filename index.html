Approach // the pattern behind the projects

I go where the problem is.

Four habits that show up in everything above, from group projects to production.

I break things until I understand why they broke.

In undergrad I was the teammate who disappeared into trial and error until it worked. Others wrote the report. I wanted to know why it failed.

I'd rather build the machine than maintain it.

Accenture taught me production discipline: releases, rollbacks, on-call. The master's was for building systems from the first line.

I want to know what's inside the model.

After building the routing layer in LLM Cross Talk, I built the tokenization pipeline to understand what that traffic was actually going to.

I rebuild what failed the first nine times.

Persistence is the habit I trust most. The tenth attempt is usually the one that holds.
